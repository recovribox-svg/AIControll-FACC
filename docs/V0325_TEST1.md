## 1. Unraveling and Chain Arrows in automatic infusion selection - feasible, done

**Why they were withheld.** The infusion selector only picks an infusion whose effect it can forecast without running
anything. Unraveling's dispel "resolves inside the game's execution"; Chain Arrows had "no read-only recipient query".

**Finding (from the game code).** Both can be forecast from data alone.
- **Unraveling** (dispel one spell buff on a blast hit, caster-level check). The game tries the target's buffs highest
  caster level first, with the action's own filters, and succeeds on d20 + caster level >= 11 + the buff's caster level.
  The AI now works that out: each removable buff's chance, in the game's order, stopping after the first success. A buff
  is worth what it does for the enemy (attack, AC, saves, concealment, mirror images, extra attacks, damage reduction)
  for the rounds it has left. One of the party's own debuffs first in line counts as a loss: the game does not skip it.
- **Chain Arrows** (the blast jumps to the nearest enemy within 10 ft, up to 1 + level/2 times, with the first attack
  roll). The blasts carry this jump themselves; the AI follows it by distance over the game's unit list, each jump at
  the first hit's chance, adjusted for spell resistance and blinking.

**Integration.** Both go through the existing bounded infusion search (at most 4 aims, 8 recipients, 24 comparisons or
8 ms a decision), so burn pricing, ownership and switching back are unchanged. Chain damage is computed once per
recipient per frame, jump data once per blast. Offline audit: 21 of 21 substance infusions supported; the jump is found
on 52 blasts.

## 2. Psionics: regaining focus and putting the trance back - done

**Before.** The focus buff has no effect of its own (it only carries what depends on it), so "Gain Psionic Focus" was
worth 0 and never used; a trance that ended with the focus stayed off.

**Now (recognised by structure, not by name).** A buff whose only parts are its start and end actions, that something of
the character depends on, is worth getting back:
- what comes with it for this character (Psionic Dodge's +1 AC, read through the "has the feat" conditions);
- 15 % of the character's round per feat that works only while it is held (Psionic Weapon / Shot / Fist and the like; at
  most 3);
- the best trance it lets the character start, for the rounds left after that trance's own action;
- for the rest of the fight (a standard-action regain loses this round).
At level 6 with Psionic Weapon and Psionic Dodge, early in a fight that is about 20 points, more than a 12-point attack;
in the last round it is less.

**Move action + standard action.** With Psionic Meditation the regain is a move action. The AI used to compare it alone
with a full attack and the full attack always won; it now compares "move action, then the standard action" with the full
round. This applies to every move-action ability (a performance started as a move action, Slayer's Study Target).

**After a fight.** The after-fight routine regains the focus ("AI recasts self buffs after fights"). A trance the AI's
own spending ended - one that was on before the AI touched it - goes back on once the focus is back ("AI manages
activatable abilities"). A trance the player had off stays off. In combat a trance is started when its own value says
so.

**Limits.** Trances built from Psionics' own code components (Weaponmaster, Dervish, Feral Warrior, Mind Knight) cannot
be read as numbers, so in combat the AI rarely spends a standard action on them; they are kept up between fights.
Feats that read the focus inside their own code (exploding criticals, aligned damage) are not counted.

## 3. Potions - audit and fixes

**Recognition.** Items in the quick slots (the belt) give the character their ability, and the AI gathers them with
the character's other abilities; a potion is always drunk by the character itself (the game's rule). The settings "use
consumables" / "use potions" decide first (`ConsumablePolicy.IsAllowed`).

**When.** Healing potions: by the heal rules and the potion HP threshold. Every other potion: only in a hard fight
(enemy threat at least 0.8 of the party's), at half its value, and only when that is worth at least 5 points after its
risks. A pure buff on the turn's main action must also beat the best immediate action by 15 %.

**Purpose, potion by potion.**

| Potion | What the AI reads | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Blur, Displacement | 20 % / 50 % miss chance, against the attacks coming at the drinker | correct |
| Invisibility | unseen by enemies without see-invisibility; ends with its first attack | correct |
| Heroism | +2 attack and saves (morale; does not add to Aid's morale) | correct |
| Haste | extra attack on a full attack, +1 attack, AC, Reflex | correct |
| Cat's Grace, Bull's Strength | +4 enhancement; nothing over a belt that gives as much | correct |
| Aid | +1 attack (morale), temporary HP | correct |
| Enlarge Person | +2 Str, -2 Dex, size (-1 attack and AC, bigger weapon dice) | correct |
| Cure Wounds | heal, by the heal rules and threshold | correct |
| Magic Weapon | +1 over the weapon's own enhancement (nothing on a +1 weapon) | correct |
| Protection from Evil / Chaos | +2 AC and saves against attackers of that alignment | correct |
| Resist Fire | only against the fire damage coming at the drinker | correct |
| **Magical Vestment (armor, shield)** | was a flat guess | **fixed**: what it adds over the armor's or shield's own enhancement (the game takes the highest); nothing without a shield |
| **Align Weapon** | was a flat guess | **fixed**: the damage reduction it gets through on the enemy attacked (e.g. a demon's DR/good) |
| **Bless Weapon** | was a flat guess | **fixed**: DR/good it gets through, and criticals confirming by themselves against an evil enemy |
| **Lesser Restoration** | never valued as a potion | **fixed**: a cleanse when the drinker is fatigued, exhausted or under a stat penalty |

**Not changed (worth discussing).** The "hard fight only" rule (threat >= 0.8 of the party's) keeps non-healing potions
for real fights. A log of a hard fight where a companion had a fitting potion and did not drink it would show whether
the threshold is right. Lesser Restoration's ability-damage healing is not counted yet.
