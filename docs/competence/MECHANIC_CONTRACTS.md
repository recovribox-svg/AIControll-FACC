## Common end-to-end contract

The modern TB and RT modes share `TacticalEngine.Decide/Execute`. `CustomBrainPatch.ShouldControlUnit` gates consciousness, player faction, direct control, main/companion settings and per-unit AI. `BuildContext` reads current native action economy and permission state; `AbilityDiscovery.Gather` combines actual actor facts, spellbooks, visible variants, conversions and quick-slot items. `UserAbilityPolicy` is checked before appraisal and on execution/restore routes. None of these boundaries can be replaced by a class-name preference.

`AddAbilityCandidates` → `EvaluateAbility` → `ScoreAbility` construct legal aims/effect forecasts. `AbilityProfile` records safe branch conditions and effect subject; `CombatMath`/`ValueModel` evaluate existing supported damage, heal, control, buffs and costs. Decide compares affordable candidates, then bounded mark/rider/boost/held-state/kinetic coupling contributes only compatible continuation value. `PreflightMainCandidates` and `CommandDispatcher.Run` apply command preparation/revalidation. `Execute` issues native commands; observers (`CastOutcome`, `DamageObserver`, `SaveObserver`, `CommandTrace`) distinguish issuing, acting, rolls, delivery and refusal. `CombatMemory` and the family lifecycle drivers retain state across redecisions.

For each replay, record: actor availability, permission, understanding, candidate/decline comparison, selected action, issued command, command act, native outcome and current lifecycle state. A passed save/miss is not command refusal; an actor unavailable or user-blocked is not an unsupported blueprint. An offline reader row does not establish any of these runtime transitions.

## Magus command, action and delivery contract

Matching native methods inspected: `MagusController.HandleUnitRunCommand`, `HandleUnitCommandDidAct`, `OnEventDidTrigger(RuleAttackWithWeapon)`, `Tick`; `UnitUseAbility.CreateCastCommand` and initialization/cast paths; `AbilityEffectStickyTouch.Apply`; `UnitPartMagus`; `UnitPartTouch`; `PreparedSpellCombat`/`PreparedSpellStrike` extension IL and native action-availability IL.

- **Initial sticky touch already works through native queuing.** `AbilityEffectStickyTouch.Apply` initializes the touch part and queues `UnitUseAbility.CreateCastCommand` with ignored cooldown. The factory can turn the held Magus touch into `UnitAttack` when Spellstrike, spell-list membership, hostile target and threat hand requirements hold. Thus issuing the initial sticky cast does not prove all Spellstrike is broken. A later chosen held charge is different: modern `Execute` creates `new UnitUseAbility` directly and does not pass through this factory.
- **Spell Combat is command sequence state.** Native `MagusController.HandleUnitRunCommand` intercepts `UnitAttack`, checks prepared spell-combat/strike opportunities, can ignore attack cooldown, chooses single/full attack, and applies the one-round Spell Combat buff. TB uses prepared state and closeness/path/five-foot rules; RT also checks cast/attack timestamps and current closeness. This cannot be forecast as an unrelated extra full-round ability.
- **Action permission is distinct from native continuation.** `BuildContext` starts from `HasStandardAction`/`HasFullRoundAction`; `AddWeaponCandidates` gates those before attack candidates. The inspected native action getters read cooldowns; they do not themselves provide a Magus exception. Consequently a native continuation opportunity can be absent from candidate representation even though a manually issued native attack would receive the controller's override. Actual actor state/replay is needed before an execution change.
- **Ranged Spellstrike carries a stored spell.** Native `UnitUseAbility` recognizes suitable Eldritch Archer spells and queues the native bow attack; the Magus part holds the spell until use. `MagusController.Tick` clears that stored spell after one round, motion, Spellstrike off or an unsuitable/no ranged first weapon. The forecast must use the actual bow delivery/shared attack and spell contribution, count real resources once, and handle unused charge loss. Existing Magus critical x2 handling alone is insufficient to establish bow-delivery accuracy.

Safe next stage: inspect actual active toggles, spellbook/list, held charge, weapon and controller opportunity without running commands. Represent one feasible cast/attack continuation on the shared scale; revalidate after the first action. Keep internal exclusions and native queue waits. Changing movement/approach policy remains outside this stage and requires author discussion.

## Future forms and native body replacement

`Polymorph.OnActivate` checks that another polymorph is not active, applies Strength/Dexterity/Constitution modifiers, size and natural armor, grants facts, and calls `UnitBody.ApplyPolymorphEffect(MainHand, OffHand, AdditionalLimbs, SecondaryAdditionalLimbs, KeepSlots)`. `OnDeactivate` removes those modifiers/facts and cancels the body effect. Master Shapeshifter adds further physical stat modifiers in the native path. `AddAdditionalLimb`, `AddSecondaryAttacks`, `EmptyHandWeaponOverride` and `ShiftersFury` are distinct inspected types; their presence is not a uniform additive-attacks contract.

The first missing future-form boundary is representation of the proposed body/gear/feature replacement. Current native body enumeration after activation remains supported. A safe forecast must use an immutable proposed description, account for removed weapons/bonuses and permitted casting, and compare transformation plus plausible remaining attacks against the best action now. It must not call `Polymorph.OnActivate`, equip fake weapons or add temporary live facts while scoring. Actual form/return-action graphs are still required to determine membership, duration, resource and unlock deltas.

## Precision, fortification and triggered attack effects

Native `RulePrepareDamage.OnTrigger` chooses Knife Master's d8 with knives and d4 otherwise, rather than d6; it also reads `UnitPartSneakAttackModifications.AdditionalDamagePerDice` and `AdditionalDamagePerRangedDice`. It requires applicable attack/target state and consumes the attack roll's sneak use. Native `RuleAttackRoll` evaluates `UnitPartFortification` for sneak, critical and Precise Strike and can negate sneak/critical on its d100 roll. Its inspected `IsAttackRollSuitableForSneakAttack` additionally suppresses bomb sneak without the corresponding native feature; no general ranged-distance defect is established by this inspection.

Current `CombatMath.ReadRoutine` records flat d6 average (`3.5 * SneakAttack`), flanked/flat-footed and precision immunity. Its `ReadHitOnTarget` fake base-damage rule reads physical decline/crit immunity, but creates no critical attack roll or fortification probability. Thus die replacement/additional dice and fortification are concrete forecast-accuracy deltas, not proof of failed native execution. `VitalStrikeExtra` already handles explicit Rowdy/mythic contributions; test those separately rather than applying all weapon sneak arithmetic blindly.

Native `AbstractWeaponTrigger` has independent gates for first attack/hit, full attack, critical/natural20, sneak, opportunity/charge, weapon category/group/range, physical form, distance, damage/kill and target/initiator subject. A shared on-hit reader cannot assume every trigger fires on every expected hit. Current simple on-hit damage/affliction and Carnage support is preserved; exact trigger-gate parity needs current concrete facts and source comparison before broadening forecasts.

## Resource and recipient lifecycle contracts

Native `ActivatableAbilityResourceLogic` distinguishes `Start`, `TurnOn`, `NewRound`, `Attack`, `AttackHit`, `AttackCrit`, `Judgment`, `AttackSpecificWeapon`, `OncePerMinute`, `OncePerHour`, `OncePerTenMinutes`, `None` and `Never`. It also has free facts and increasing/decreasing-cost facts. Inspected `ActivatableAbility.ResourceCount` is resource amount/item charges, not affordable rounds. Current `ToggleValue` treats that quantity as rounds; `StopIfPointless` treats resource presence generically as continuing drain. The correction must separately price starting versus continuing, already paid activation, specific event rates, modifiers and expiry; supplied kinetic exports contain no positive resource-bearing performance/judgment graph to establish prevalence.

Native `ForbidSpellCasting.OnTurnOn` checks `IgnoreFeature` on the actual affected owner. Current `BuffPartsReader` keeps a `ForbidsSpells` kind without that exemption. The performer's toggle profile separately checks its native ignore feature, while the shared recipient appraisal can still reject an accepting caster's buff without checking the recipient's exemption. This is a structural recipient contract concern; need an actual graph/actor to establish an affected current consumer.

Native `PreciseStrike` requires a flanked melee weapon attack plus Solo Tactics or another engaged actor with the matching feat. Current caster-granted feat provenance is a preservation result, but its teamwork benefit's half-weight is a model approximation. It does not prove that a partner is present, eligible or can act before expiry. A future correction should reuse decision-local party/recipient facts, not expand a whole build search.

## Performance and safe-query contract for scheduled work

The source currently bounds/reuses native attack and damage probes, parameter/context reads and class-mechanic searches. Preserve `CombatMath.ParamsOf`, target-free `DamageContext` eligibility and observer counters. No new runtime search, movement policy, cache, limit or threshold was added by this audit.

For each future sensitive batch, specify scenario dimensions before profiling: actors/enemies/recipient count, actual abilities/variants, attack lines, active graph nodes, states and chain length. Record cold versus repeated decision cost, end-to-end latency distribution, allocations/GC and native query counts against matched current test-2 saves. Use observed baseline variability to decide acceptance; do not invent millisecond promises from actorless fixtures.

Prefer immutable component descriptions keyed by actual blueprint identity with explicit invalidation on asset reload. Target/resource/fact/equipment/policy-dependent scores belong in a single decision or a key with every relevant revision. Resource quantities, acceptance and native action opportunities cannot be retained merely by fight/round. Never cache shared mutable MechanicsContexts, live command objects or hypothetical equipment/fact mutation. If a complete safe key or nonmutating query is unavailable, document the missing adapter and scenario; do not conceal it behind a class preference, larger score, fresh search cap or quality reduction.
